# Brand System — Chinese Teaching Video System

## Brand Identity

### Character IP: 熊猫老师 (Panda Teacher)
- **Role**: Main instructor for all Chinese teaching videos
- **Style**: Cute, educational, friendly cartoon panda
- **Consistency**: Fixed character sheet used across all prompts (see `config/character_sheet.md`)

### Color Palette
| Usage | Light | Dark | Hex |
|-------|-------|------|-----|
| Primary | Bamboo green | #8faa6e | Nature, growth, Chinese culture |
| Secondary | Warm gold | #c49a2a | Premium, education, achievement |
| Background | Warm cream | #faf9f7 | Clean, readable, friendly |
| Text | Charcoal brown | #3d3530 | Warm, not harsh black |
| Accent | Sky blue | N/A | Highlights, interactive elements |

### Typography
- **Headers**: Instrument Serif — editorial, distinctive
- **Body**: Manrope — clean, modern, excellent CJK rendering
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono — technical clarity

### Visual Guidelines
- All videos: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 30fps, H.264
- Subtitles: Chinese characters + pinyin (above) + English (below)
- Never let AI video platforms generate text — overlay in post-production
- Consistent intro/outro with character animation

### Tone of Voice
- **Friendly**: Conversational, warm, encouraging
- **Clear**: Short sentences, simple vocabulary
- **Educational**: Structured teaching progression (intro → lesson → practice → review)
- **Culturally Aware**: Reference Chinese culture naturally, not forced

### YouTube Branding
- Channel name: Z TURNS Chinese
- Thumbnail style: Character + large text (3-5 Chinese chars) + color background
- Video titles: "[Chinese Word] - What it really means | Learn Chinese in 60s"
- Consistent upload schedule for algorithm optimization
